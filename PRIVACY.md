# Privacy Policy - MIR MEDIA LABS

_Last updated: October 4, 2026 - MirCorp_

**MIR MEDIA LABS collects no personal data. There are no analytics, telemetry, ads or trackers.**

- **Your files stay on your PC.** Prompts, references and generated media are stored only in the app's `data/` folder on your computer.
- **AI runs locally.** Rendering uses ComfyUI and Ollama on your own machine.
- **The Android app** talks only to the Media Lab server address *you* enter. It stores that address and your access key on the device.
- **Optional network use, only when you trigger it:**
  - downloading models or LoRAs (for example from Hugging Face or Civitai, under their own privacy policies)
  - checking for app updates
- **Bug reports and feedback** open *your* email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. Bug reports include the app version and your device or browser model, so you can review and edit them first.
- Emails sent to mirmedialabs@gmail.com are used only to respond to you and improve the app. They are never sold or shared.

Questions: mirmedialabs@gmail.com
