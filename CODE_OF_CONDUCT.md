# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, be constructive, and no harassment of any kind. The same goes for content: don't use this project's spaces to share illegal content or non-consensual imagery of real people.

Report problems to **mirmedialabs@gmail.com**. Maintainers may remove content and ban participants who break these rules.
